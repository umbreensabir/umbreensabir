🔗 [PCPs](https://github.com/umbreensabir/PCPs)

### OpenMP Performance & Energy Tuning
During my MSc Informatics at the **Technical University of Munich**, I developed an OpenMP tuning plugin in C/C++ within the **Periscope Tuning Framework (PTF)**.

The plugin explored different OpenMP thread-count configurations and evaluated their effect on application execution time and energy efficiency.

### HPC Benchmarking & Performance Data
At TU Dresden, I also developed a Python-based workflow for HPC benchmarking and performance-data collection using **SLURM**, **Python**, and **MariaDB/MySQL**.

The workflow automated job submission, output parsing, storage of benchmark results, and comparison with cluster-monitoring data.
